NEREID

System frame in progress. A NEREID river in Alpine River Valley: flow through bends, foam and whitewater over rapids, rocks in the stream — captured with LENS without editor UI; ideally a short video.

What is inside

Water data

Authoritative water body data and level and current sampling from ECS.

Buoyancy

Buoyancy and drag forces for any genre.

GPU flow

A local river flow field in a texture: waves, foam and whitewater follow the channel bends, independent of the camera.

Geometry and rendering

Reusable surface geometry, a renderer bridge, planar reflections, riverbed refraction.

Foam and whitewater

Foam, whitewater on fast currents, quality profiles.

River confluences

Flow at river confluences in ECS and in the scene.

Ocean

Ocean tiles with ripples, LOD for large worlds.

Editor

River grade and current are edited in Studio through the native bridge.

What it lets you do

  • Build rivers, lakes and ocean with one data model for gameplay and visuals.
  • Swim, sink and drift with the current — in RTS, FPS or any other game.
  • Show flow, foam and whitewater that follow the channel bends.
  • Edit river grade and current in the editor and erode banks together with TerraWeave.
