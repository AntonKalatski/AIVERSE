LENS camera behaviours and presets#

Every view camera in AIVERSE comes from one set of camera bodies (CameraSphereOrbit.h holds the sphere orbit) in engine/render/CameraBehavior.h, and the ECS camera rig (CameraRigComponent, engine/render/CameraRig.h) runs the same math. A project picks a body by the camera block it writes and starts from a named preset; there are no per-game camera classes.

BodyBlockWhat it does
overheadgame.json "camera"Free map camera: projection, pitch, yaw, lens, zoom limits, pixel-perfect ortho zoom
followgame.json topDown.camera, a map's "camera"Keeps a focus point in frame: smoothing, look-ahead along the move and the aim, speed zoom, velocity lead, map bounds, lens and clip planes
chaserules / map "camera": {"chase": {...}}Behind and above a heading (vehicles): distance, height, pitch, lead, lens
first personrules / map "camera": {"firstPerson": {...}}Eyes with mouse look: sensitivity (and while aiming), pitch limit, lens
sphere orbitplanet maps (engine/render/CameraSphereOrbit.h)Orbit over a sphere's surface, zooming out to the whole sphere and the system; parallel-transported heading (no poles), smooth focus changes, a view frame with the focus at the origin

Presets#

engine/render/CameraPresets.h holds the named presets as JSON. A block names one and overrides any key:

"camera": {"preset": "overhead-pixel"}
"topDown": {"camera": {"preset": "follow-speed", "distance": 20}}
"camera": {"chase": {"preset": "chase-low"}, "firstPerson": {"preset": "first-person", "sensitivity": 0.0005}}
PresetBodyFraming
overheadoverheadPerspective map camera, 54° pitch, rotatable
overhead-flatoverheadSteeper perspective, fixed rotation
overhead-pixeloverheadTop-down orthographic, 32 px per cell (sprite games)
overhead-isometricoverheadOrthographic 35.264° / 45° (true isometric)
followfollowThird-person top-down follow with stick and aim look-ahead
follow-overheadfollowStraight-down follow, short look-ahead
follow-speedfollowStraight-down follow that pulls back and leads with speed
follow-telephotofollowNarrow 20° lens from far away, fog scaled to the lens
chasechaseThe default vehicle camera (11 m behind, 1.4 m up)
chase-lowchaseCloser and lower, wider lens
first-personfirst personThe default first-person look

Unknown preset names are reported by resolveCameraPreset and the block's own keys are used. Adding a preset is a data change in CameraPresets.h; the keys are those of the body's block.

Studio and MCP#

lens_camera_presets (MCP / Studio agent) lists every preset with its body and keys, or one by name. The Studio cameras window has a Follow Body section: it switches a camera to the follow body and applies any follow preset; the fields stay editable after that.

ECS rig modes#

CameraRigComponent::mode: Static, LookAt, Follow, Orbit, Rail, Chase, FirstPerson. Chase uses distance, pitch, offset.y (height), offset.z (lead) and yaw (turn around the heading); FirstPerson puts the eye at the target plus offset (turning with the target) and looks along the target's +Z with yaw / pitch added. A Follow rig with a CameraFollowComponent (cameras.json "follow" block, presets allowed, "bounds": [minX, minZ, maxX, maxZ]) runs the follow body itself: angle, distance, smoothing, velocity lead, speed zoom, lens and bounds, the same code as the game follow camera. Shake (CameraShakeComponent) and the physical lens (CameraLensComponent) apply on top of every mode. Test: camera_behavior (presets, every body, ECS Chase / FirstPerson against the view chase camera).
