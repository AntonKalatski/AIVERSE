PHYSIS

System frame in progress. A moment where the environment acts on its own: wind driving a fire through grass, or a structure failing under stress. Visible consequences, not a debug heatmap.

What is inside

Environment core

A deterministic fixed-point atmosphere and surface grid queried from ECS.

Climate and weather

Climate zones, weather fronts and seasons.

Surface

Snow, wetness and ice; friction and runoff hints for gameplay.

Disasters

Fire and fuel, shallow floods, earthquakes.

Wind, gravity, storms

3D wind zones with gusts and shelter, gravity fields, hurricanes and tornadoes.

Editor and network

The PHYSIS window, in-scene fields with gizmos, authoritative server reconciliation and a command journal.

What it lets you do

  • Add weather, fire and floods to a game of any genre with one system.
  • Push physics bodies with wind and change gravity — through a separate Box3D adapter.
  • Author wind zones, storms and gravity in map JSON and edit them right in the scene.
  • Keep environment fields in sync on the server: reconciliation, command replay, recovery after a restart.
